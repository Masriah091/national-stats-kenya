Data & Sources

Nothing on this site is invented. Every number names the institution that published it and the year it describes. Where two respected sources disagree (for example the Central Bank's agent-based mobile-money total versus Safaricom's platform total), both are shown and the difference is explained.

    Methodology

    • Census vs estimate: 2009 and 2019 population figures are census counts (KNBS). 2023–2024 population figures are World Bank estimates.
    • Money: GDP is shown both in current US dollars (World Bank) and in Kenyan shillings at current prices (KNBS). Dollar figures move with the exchange rate as well as with output.
    • Crops: tea and coffee are reported for crop years where the source says so; tonnages are "made tea" (processed), not green leaf.
    • Mobile money: the Central Bank total counts transactions through agents; Safaricom's total counts every transaction on the M-Pesa platform. They measure different things.
    • Climate: station figures are long-term means, not a single year's weather. A single year can differ sharply from the mean.
    • Derived figures: the 15–64 age share (60.2%) is the arithmetic residual of the two published World Bank shares (0–14: 36.8%; 65+: 3.0%).
    • What is left out: county rainfall is shown only for Nairobi and Mombasa, where a published station mean was found. No county figure was estimated or filled in.

    About this site

    Kenya in Numbers is an independent statistics portal built for the MakeFun "Best National Statistics Site" bounty. It is not affiliated with the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics, the Central Bank of Kenya, Safaricom, or any government body.

    It runs entirely offline: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, system fonts only, no external requests, no tracking. All charts are drawn in the browser from the figures in data.js.

    Figures were checked against their published sources on 26 November 2026. If a source revises a figure, the number on this site becomes out of date — check the source list above for the publisher and year behind any number you quote.
