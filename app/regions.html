Counties & Climate

47 counties under the 2010 constitution, from Nairobi's 4.4 million people to Lamu's 143,920 — and a climate that runs from 250 mm of rain a year in the arid north to 2,500 mm in the wet highlands.

Climate in brief

    Rain falls in two seasons — the long rains (March–May) and the short rains (October–December) — with the coast wettest in May.
