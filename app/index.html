    Three numbers that explain Kenya

    • Tea: 598,500 tonnes of made tea in 2024 — exports earned KSh 181.69 billion. (KNBS / Tea Board of Kenya)
    • Mobile money: KSh 8.7 trillion moved in 2024; 82.3% of adults use it. (Central Bank of Kenya)
    • Youth: 36.8% of the population is under 15; median age 20.8. (World Bank, 2024)
